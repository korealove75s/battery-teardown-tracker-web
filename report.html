1

Data files

OCV Tracking SheetRequired

Mass Production E&L Grade OCV Tracking Sheet (.xlsx) — Master E & L sheet and each cell's tracking sheet. If the Master sheet has Shape · Coating Location · Long side · Short Side · Height columns, they are used directly.

Analysis reportOptional

Frozen IR · Spot analysis in the report layout (e.g. test1.xlsx, or Export Excel from the tracker's Analysis tab). Supplies Location (inside / outside), Shape and reviewed values. Not needed when the OCV Tracking Sheet already has these columns; when both are loaded, a value in this report wins, and cells missing here keep the OCV Tracking Sheet's value.

Lot production & defect summaryRequired

Per PKG lot: E-grade %, L-grade %, production, E count, HM, L count (e.g. test2.xlsx). Drives the monthly trend and defect rates.

Process genealogy CSVOptional

Cell → mixing / coating / roll press / slitting / DNC / AZS / packaging export (e.g. 9.241.csv). Needed for the AZS/DNC and electrode slides.
